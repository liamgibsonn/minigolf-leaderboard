Sound effects for the big screen's pop-ups (MP3 files). Which file plays when is set at
the top of public/popups.js (GOOD_STEPS and BAILED_STEPS).

As a combo of good results (birdies, eagles, holes in one) stacks up in a pop-up:

  1st to 4th result   pinball0.mp3
  5th (the jackpot)   scratch4.mp3
  6th and later       silent

Maxed-out holes have no sounds yet.

Any file that's missing just stays silent. Open the big screen with /?popup-preview on
the end of the address to loop a sample 5 combo and 8 combo while you try sounds out.
