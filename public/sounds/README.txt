Sound effects for the big screen's pop-ups (MP3 files). Which file plays when is set at
the top of public/popups.js (GOOD_STEPS, BAILED_STEPS and TYPE_SOUNDS).

As a combo of good results (birdies, eagles, holes in one) stacks up in a pop-up:

  1st to 4th result   pinball.mp3
  5th (the jackpot)   pinball.mp3 and choir.mp3 together
  6th and later       silent

Eagle pop-ups go like this:

  first, nothing on screen yet   eaglein.mp3
  as the card appears            eagle.mp3
  shortly after                  the animals pop in with the pinballs (choir on the 5th)
  as the card leaves             eagleout.mp3

Maxed-out holes have no sounds yet. Any file that's missing just stays silent. Open the
big screen with /?popup-preview on the end of the address to loop sample pop-ups.
